# DOMAIN MODEL

## UNIVERSE

**7d-space.** Each dimension is an axis from 0-999 that loops around. Biggest possible granularity gives us 1000^7 = 10^21 possible locations which is enormous. Max shortest distance between 2 points however is suprisingly small: math.sqrt (700x500^2) which is ~13229 units. Idea being: space is enormous and impossible to be very full yet any information in it is at all times theoretically reachable. [While still being able to hold much more information that ever is explorable.] *Idea: Everything, Everywhere at the same time, at any game-state there's a potential to be connected to any other.*

This 7d space is used as a map for generic information that enables emergent and decaying data-structures. It will constantly keep re-mapping itself to the space so **we're not keeping track of space itself, as that's indredible huge, we're keeping track of the information that lives in it.*** This, with various levels of granularity [LOD] which ARE tied to the 7D-grid. So at any time we determine in this 7D space which areas are considered HIGH,MEDIUM or LOW [LOD] and aggregate all member information accordingly. []

# FIELDS

FIELDS govern potentials in the universe. 

Abstract: 

Mathematical: whole universe returns 1 for every 7D-location being queried [for any of its sub-functions, they all produce similar scoped results), as long as a field doesn't alter them.] [In other words, the baseline universe will not need to know any functions, and it will without fail answer everything with 1. (very symbolic)] So without a field existing no matter what you'll ask of it, it will always return 1. So unless specific FIELD-functions are overwriting this universal constant [UC=1] they will be expected to work on 1. [so 'meaningful' results from working on [UC] could be scoped between 0 and 2 (so we always have a polarity to work with, smaller 1 and bigger than 1, both with a range of 1 to measure severity in either direction)]
